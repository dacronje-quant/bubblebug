# Trying the home neighbourhood and rewards

Download this branch as a ZIP, extract it, and open `try-rewards.html` in a browser. No install or build is needed. This preview starts at home with 250 stars, 65 hearts, all 12 family cats, boss presents and the homecoming complete, so you can try the post-game maze. It keeps progress in memory, so reloading starts the preview again and your normal adventure stays intact. Open `index.html` for normal saved play.

The title picker has three modes: **Easy** adds jumping and landing help; **Medium** is the former Easy; **Hard** keeps its original movement and happy suns. Existing Easy adventures carry over to Medium. Difficulty remains a device setting, including when trying the preview.

1. Walk right out of the house. Follow the lower path through **Front Garden → Pond Walk → Root Hollow → Sparkle Gardens**. The camera should follow continuously, and no jump is needed. Walk back home.
2. Try the leaf steps in the Front Garden and the upper branch route. In Root Hollow, bubble both sleepy buds and collect the bonus stars from their nook.
3. Walk left into the **Secret Garden**: an overgrown hedge labyrinth with loops and dead ends. Move with all four arrows/WASD, a gamepad D-pad, or the four on-screen arrows. Touch three paw stones, then find Rainbow in the upper-left corner. Choose Marshmallow, Phoebe or Rainbow as your playable kitten. Leave through the home picture, or move right at the lower-right entrance. Return to the mirror's new cat tab to change kitten again.
4. Stand on the mirror's paw ring. From the first item row press Up to focus categories, Left/Right to change category, and Down to return to items. Space/X/Enter equips. A first tap on an unselected item previews it; another equips it if unlocked. Star totals never decrease. Locked items show a progress bar and stars still needed; collect 25–250 stars to unlock extras. Try the scuba mask with a hat/collar and the smaller paw trail.
5. In the Front Garden, stand on the critter-picture paw ring and invite a friend for one heart. Invite a second species, then walk through the neighbourhood and pet them. Reusing an invitation and petting should never charge or award hearts.
6. At the living-room fountain, spend one heart once. Watch six seconds of rainbow water jets, flying hearts, confetti, fireworks, party music and family dancing. The new twirl picture appears after the celebration. Step away, then repeat for free. Holding a button should produce one celebration.
7. Open `index.html`, choose each difficulty from the title screen, and try jumping and a gloomy bump. Medium should feel like the old Easy; only Hard loses happy suns. Pause → home lets you change the mode and Continue.
8. In normal play, check that the left maze gate remains locked until BOTH the homecoming and all 12 cats are complete. Equip an unlocked extra, reload, and Continue. Your kitten, items, collected stars, friends, map ★ marks and completion stay earned. Older star purchases remain usable even below the new milestone. Maze paw stones and current position survive a reload.

The additions have automated checks for saves, every star threshold, real scene inputs, physical routes, puzzles, difficulty and roaming residents. `node tools/test-maze.js` checks every maze corridor and exit plus real walking inputs, post-game access, kitten choices and reloads. Chromium checks cover keyboard/mouse input, file launch, reloading, preview save protection and emulated tablet touch. Canvas scenes are rendered for inspection. A physical gamepad/tablet playtest remains useful for judging feel and touch comfort.

Family wishes, house decorating, jukebox tunes and a play corner remain deferred. No recovery benches or voice lines were added.
