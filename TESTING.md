# Trying the home neighbourhood and rewards

Download this branch as a ZIP, extract it, and open `try-rewards.html` in a browser. No install or build is needed. This preview starts at home with 250 stars, 65 hearts, family and boss presents. It keeps progress in memory, so reloading starts the preview again and your normal adventure stays intact. Open `index.html` for normal saved play.

The title picker has three modes: **Easy** adds jumping and landing help; **Medium** is the former Easy; **Hard** keeps its original movement and happy suns. Existing Easy adventures carry over to Medium. Difficulty remains a device setting, including when trying the preview.

1. Walk right out of the house. Follow the lower path through **Front Garden → Pond Walk → Root Hollow → Sparkle Gardens**. The camera should follow continuously, and no jump is needed. Walk back home.
2. Try the leaf steps in the Front Garden and the upper branch route. In Root Hollow, bubble both sleepy buds and collect the bonus stars from their nook.
3. Walk left out of the house into the **Pawprint Maze**. Climb its terraces, touch the three glowing paw pads, and find the star nook above. The lower exit back home is always open.
4. Stand on the mirror's paw ring. Try the clothes and bubble-picture tabs. Tap once to preview, again to buy; selected items show star pips and affordable pictures glow. Owned choices can be reused freely. Try the jingle collar while running and flower bubbles while playing.
5. In the Front Garden, stand on the critter-picture paw ring and invite a friend for one heart. Invite a second species, then walk through the neighbourhood and pet them. Reusing an invitation and petting should never charge or award hearts.
6. At the living-room fountain, spend one heart to unlock its fireworks, family dance and twirl. Step away, then use it again: every repeat is free. Holding a button should produce one celebration.
7. Open `index.html`, choose each difficulty from the title screen, and try jumping and a gloomy bump. Medium should feel like the old Easy; only Hard loses happy suns. Pause → home lets you change the mode and Continue.
8. In normal play, buy an extra, reload, and Continue. The item and remaining wallet should persist, while collected stars, friends and map ★ marks remain earned.

The additions have automated checks for saves, real scene inputs, physical routes, puzzles, purchases, difficulty and roaming residents. Chromium checks cover keyboard/mouse input, file launch, reloading, preview save protection and emulated tablet touch. Canvas scenes are also rendered for inspection. A physical gamepad/tablet playtest remains useful for judging feel and touch comfort.

Companions, family wishes, house decorating, jukebox tunes and a play corner are deferred. This version focuses on simple optional rewards and a connected home area. No recovery benches or voice lines were added.
